An account is a ledger location. A transaction always moves an amount from one
account to another, so choosing the right account type determines how Maille
interprets the movement.

Five types answer **where value is**: bank accounts, cash, investment
accounts, assets, and liabilities. Two types answer **why value changed**:
expenses and revenue.

## Book value and estimation

Two account types carry a second number besides their balance. Investment
positions and assets accept dated observations of their worth: unit prices for
positions, valuations for assets. The latest observation becomes the
estimation, shown next to the balance with its observation date and its
unrealized difference. An estimation records what the world says; it never
produces a transaction. See
[Investment accounts](/docs/financial/account-types/investment-accounts) and
[Assets](/docs/financial/account-types/assets).
