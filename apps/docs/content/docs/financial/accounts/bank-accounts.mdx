---
title: Bank accounts
description: Money an institution holds, checked against the statements it sends.
---

A bank account is money held by an institution that keeps its own records of
it. That outside record is why the type exists: a bank account is the part of
the ledger you expect to verify against evidence imported from elsewhere.

## Movement tracking

Bank accounts are created with movement tracking enabled. Each statement line
imported into Maille becomes a movement, and a movement stays separate from
its financial explanation until you link the two. The balance is derived from
transactions, so agreement between the balance and the imported statements is
a result you checked, not an assumption.

## Opening balance

A starting balance describes the account at the ledger's starting date.
Maille tracks history from that date forward, and the starting balance anchors
it.

## When to use it

Use a bank account for anything a third party can confirm: a current account,
a savings account, a broker that issues statements. Rename the bootstrapped
example to the institution you actually use.

A broker account you hold positions in is better as an
[Investment account](/docs/financial/accounts/investment-accounts), and
money with no statement behind it belongs in
[Cash](/docs/financial/accounts/cash).

## Implications of each action

- **Import a statement** — movements are evidence only. Nothing is spent,
  earned, or moved in the ledger until you link the lines to activities.
- **Link a movement** — the attributed amount counts toward both records'
  completion: the movement completes when links cover its amount, the activity
  when links cover its transaction totals on tracked accounts.
- **Edit the starting balance** — shifts every derived balance and report for
  the account, at every date. A boundary correction, not a fix for today's
  mismatch: find the missing transaction instead.
- **Disable movement tracking** — activities touching the account can complete
  without statements. The balance stops being proven, which is exactly what
  the setting controls.
- **Delete the account** — removes its statement history and every
  transaction leg referencing it. See
  [Accounts](/docs/financial/accounts) before doing so.
