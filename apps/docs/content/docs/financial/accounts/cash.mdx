---
title: Cash
description: Money held directly, with no statement to reconcile against.
---

Cash is money in a wallet, a drawer, or wherever only you can count it. No
institution records it, so there is nothing to reconcile against: cash
activities are complete without bank evidence, and the bootstrapped Cash
account is created without movement tracking.

## Why the type exists

Cash is as real as bank money, and its balance is derived from the same
transactions. What differs is verification. A bank balance can be checked
against a statement; a cash balance cannot. Separating the two lets Maille
require evidence where evidence exists and stay quiet where it does not.

## Usage

A withdrawal moves value from a bank account to cash; a purchase paid in cash
moves value from cash to an expense account. The balance tells you how much
physical money you hold at any date.
