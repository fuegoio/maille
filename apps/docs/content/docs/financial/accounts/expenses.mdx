---
title: Expenses
description: The destination for consumed value.
---

An expense account is the destination for consumed value: groceries, rent,
fees. The type exists to answer the question a balance cannot: why did value
decrease. Money is never located in an expense account; it has already left
through it.

## Transfers do not touch it

A transaction between two balance accounts is a transfer. Neither side is an
expense, so moving money between your own accounts is never reported as
spending. Consumption is booked exactly when a transaction names an expense
account, and the derived activity types follow the same rule: classification
cannot contradict the ledger.

## Where they appear

Depreciation installments are booked to an expense account. One Expense
account is created for you; add as many as your reporting needs, and use
[categories](/docs/financial/categories-projects-and-sharing) to group them.
