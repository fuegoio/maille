---
title: Accounts
description: The places value is held, owed, earned, and spent.
---

An account is a ledger location. A transaction always moves an amount from one
account to another, so choosing the right account type determines how Maille
interprets the movement.

## Account types

| Type                                                                 | Role in the model                            | Typical examples                 |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| [`bank_account`](/docs/financial/accounts/bank-accounts)             | Money held by a financial institution        | Current account, savings account |
| [`investment_account`](/docs/financial/accounts/investment-accounts) | Invested value and available investment cash | Brokerage account                |
| [`cash`](/docs/financial/accounts/cash)                              | Money held directly                          | Wallet, cash envelope            |
| [`assets`](/docs/financial/accounts/assets)                          | Non-cash value you own                       | Car, home, equipment             |
| [`liabilities`](/docs/financial/accounts/liabilities)                | Amounts you owe                              | Loan, credit balance             |
| [`expense`](/docs/financial/accounts/expenses)                       | Destination for consumed value               | Groceries, rent, fees            |
| [`revenue`](/docs/financial/accounts/revenues)                       | Source of earned value                       | Salary, interest, refund         |

The balance, investment, asset, and liability types answer **where value is**.
Expense and revenue accounts answer **why value changed**. A transfer between
two balance accounts changes location without becoming income or expense.

Each type has its own page, listed under this one.

## Defaults

When a user is created, Maille bootstraps Revenue, Expense, Cash, Assets,
Liabilities, Bank account, and Investment account records. The first five are
marked as defaults; bank and investment accounts are examples that you can
rename or replace with the institutions you actually use.

## Opening balances

A starting balance describes the account at the ledger's starting date. It is
not a synthetic transaction. This keeps imported history honest: Maille knows
where tracking begins instead of inventing an unexplained event before it.

Investment-style accounts can also carry a starting cash balance so available
cash remains distinct from total account value.

<Callout type="warning">
  Opening balances and the ledger starting date define the historical boundary. Enter them before
  relying on balance or fund reports.
</Callout>

## Movement tracking

The `movements` setting controls whether an account is expected to reconcile
against external statement lines. Enable it for bank and broker accounts you
import. Leave it disabled for conceptual accounts such as Expense or Revenue,
which have no external statement of their own.

## Book value and estimation

Balance is what the ledger says. Investment accounts and asset accounts can
also carry an estimation: what dated observations say the value is worth now.
The estimation appears next to the balance, with its observation date and its
unrealized difference, and is never booked into the ledger. See
[Investment accounts](/docs/financial/accounts/investment-accounts) and
[Assets](/docs/financial/accounts/assets).

## Implications of each action

| Action                   | Implication                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Create an account        | The type is chosen now and decides the derived activity types of every transaction touching it. Pick the type for the financial role, not the label.                                                                  |
| Rename an account        | Labels only. Reports follow the record, so nothing else changes.                                                                                                                                                      |
| Edit a starting balance  | A boundary correction: every derived balance and report for the account shifts for all dates. Never use it to paper over a present-day difference — differences belong to transactions.                               |
| Toggle movement tracking | An evidence contract: enabling makes touching activities incomplete until movements cover them; disabling lets them complete without evidence.                                                                        |
| Delete an account        | Deletes with it every transaction leg referencing it, its movements, positions, assets, counterparties, and fund allocations. The most destructive action in Maille — prefer renaming or disabling movement tracking. |

Every action above recomputes balances, fund positions, and statuses
automatically; the table lists what cannot be undone. The complete reference,
including activities, movements, funds, and valuations, is
[Actions & implications](/docs/financial/actions).
