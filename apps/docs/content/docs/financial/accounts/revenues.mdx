---
title: Revenues
description: The source of earned value.
---

A revenue account is the source of earned value: salary, interest, a refund.
The type exists to answer why value increased. Money is never located in a
revenue account; it has already arrived through it.

## Realized income only

Revenue records value that actually reached you. A position whose market
value rose, or an asset whose appraisal came back higher, produced no income:
those observations live in the estimation layer and never become transactions.
The gain is realized when you sell, as a normal activity. Keeping market
moves out of revenue is what makes income history mean what it says.

## Where they appear

Activity types are derived from the legs, so an activity with a revenue leg
is reported as income with the amount its transactions carry. Use
[categories](/docs/financial/categories-projects-and-sharing) to group
revenues for reporting.

## Implications of each action

- **Recording a refund or reimbursement** moves value from a revenue account
  to the account that received it. It counts as a revenue flow by derivation
  — if you would rather it net against an expense, book the transaction
  against the expense account instead.
- **Deleting a revenue account** deletes the transactions that arrived
  through it, removing those amounts from income history. Prefer renaming.
- **Market gains never appear here.** No action on prices or valuations can
  create revenue; only a sale, recorded as an activity, realizes a gain.
