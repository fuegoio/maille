title: Actions & implications
description: What every Maille action does to the ledger, what recomputes, and what to watch for.
---

Every action in Maille lands on the same few tables and triggers the same
derivations. This page is the reference for what each action **changes**, what
**recomputes automatically**, and what **cannot be undone** or needs care.
Concepts are covered on their own pages; here they are compressed into
implications.

## Reading this page

- **Ledger effect** — what changes in the stored records.
- **Derived effect** — what recomputes without any further action. Balances,
  activity types and amounts, statuses, fund positions, and shared liability
  all derive at read time, so an edit is never "half-applied".
- Rows about deletion describe cascades as implemented: deleting a parent
  record removes or detaches its dependents, exactly as listed.

## Accounts

| Action                               | Ledger effect                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Derived effect and cautions                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Create an account                    | Adds a ledger location with a type, optional starting balances, and a movement-tracking flag.                                                                                                                                                                | Balances start from the starting balances you entered. Choose the type now: it decides the derived activity types of every future transaction touching it.                                                             |
| Rename an account                    | Changes a label.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Nothing financial moves. Reports follow automatically because records reference the account, not its name.                                                                                                             |
| Change an account's starting balance | Changes the opening boundary condition.                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Every balance, report, and fund position derived from that account shifts for all dates. Do this only to correct the boundary, not to fix a present-day difference — differences belong to transactions.               |
| Enable movement tracking             | The account now expects external evidence.                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Activities touching it become **incomplete** until linked movements cover their transaction totals. Existing activities change status immediately.                                                                     |
| Disable movement tracking            | The account no longer requires evidence.                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Activities touching only this account can complete without movements. Use for accounts you cannot import statements for.                                                                                               |
| Delete an account                    | Cascades: deletes its transactions (whole rows for every leg referencing it), its movements, its positions and investments (with their prices), its assets (with valuations and depreciation plans), its counterparties, sharing rows, and fund allocations. | Every dependent number recomputes from what remains. **This is the most destructive action in Maille** — history attached to the account leaves with it. Prefer renaming or disabling movement tracking over deleting. |
| Share an account                     | Records a sharing relationship with a contact, with a role and proportion.                                                                                                                                                                                   | Nothing until transactions use it: shared liability derives from transaction legs, not from the stored proportion.                                                                                                     |

<Callout type="warning">
  Deleting an account deletes its transaction history — including legs of activities you may think
  of as belonging elsewhere. Merge or rename instead when history must survive.
</Callout>

## Activities and transactions

| Action                                                 | Ledger effect                                                                                 | Derived effect and cautions                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Create an activity                                     | Adds the event: name, date, category, project. With no transactions it derives as neutral.    | A future date makes it a prevision (scheduled, counted nowhere). Every dependent number updates the moment transactions are added.                                                                                                     |
| Add a transaction                                      | Adds one double-entry movement, with optional asset, investment, counterparty, and fund legs. | Balances on both accounts, the activity's derived types and per-type amounts, statuses, investment quantities, and fund positions all recompute. Fund legs may not exceed the transaction amount.                                      |
| Edit a transaction's amount or accounts                | Changes the movement itself.                                                                  | Every dependent derivation follows, including historical balances at past dates. Reconciliation totals change, so linked movements may no longer cover them — the activity goes back to **incomplete** until the evidence is adjusted. |
| Edit a transaction's investment leg (quantity)         | Changes the units the leg moves.                                                              | Held quantities recompute from initial quantity plus legs; estimations follow. Money (amount) and units (quantity) are separate facts — keep both honest.                                                                              |
| Add or edit fund legs                                  | Changes which purposes the transaction's money serves.                                        | Fund balances and per-account positions recompute. Legs beyond the transaction amount are rejected.                                                                                                                                    |
| Delete a transaction                                   | Removes that movement only.                                                                   | The activity's types, amounts, and status recompute; other transactions and all movements survive.                                                                                                                                     |
| Delete an activity                                     | Cascades: deletes its transactions and its reconciliation links.                              | Movements survive, but lose their links and return to **incomplete** — they are evidence without an explanation again.                                                                                                                 |
| Change an activity's date                              | Moves the ledger date of all its transactions.                                                | Balances, period reports, and scheduled status recompute. Crossing "today" turns a prevision into fact.                                                                                                                                |
| Change an activity's category, subcategory, or project | Changes reporting labels only.                                                                | No balance, type, or status changes. Classification can never contradict the ledger, and relabeling never rewrites financial history.                                                                                                  |

## Movements and reconciliation

| Action                                          | Ledger effect                                                 | Derived effect and cautions                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Import movements                                | Adds statement lines as evidence. No ledger entry exists yet. | Nothing is spent or earned in the ledger until you link. Duplicates (same account, name, calendar day, amount within 0.05) are flagged in the preview.                                                                                                                                |
| Edit a movement                                 | Corrects what the institution reported.                       | Reconciliation comparisons re-run against the new amount or account.                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| Link a movement to an activity (with an amount) | Creates a reconciliation link carrying the attributed amount. | The movement approaches completion as its linked amounts sum to its statement amount; the activity approaches completion as movement totals cover its transaction totals on movement-tracked accounts. Partial amounts are how one statement line is split across several activities. |
| Edit or remove a link amount                    | Changes the attributed amount.                                | Both records' statuses recompute immediately.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Delete a movement                               | Cascades its links.                                           | Activities that relied on it go back to **incomplete**. The ledger entries themselves are untouched — the proof disappears, not the fact.                                                                                                                                             |

## Funds

| Action                   | Ledger effect                                                                                                                          | Derived effect and cautions                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Create a fund            | Adds a purpose with a start date, color, and tree position.                                                                            | With no allocations or legs it has a zero balance and changes nothing else.                                                                                                                      |
| Set opening allocations  | Claims parts of accounts' opening balances for the fund, effective at its start date.                                                  | Rejected when an allocation exceeds what the account supports, or when the fund's start date would fall after transactions already allocated to it. Positions replay: the money becomes tracked. |
| Edit a fund's start date | Re-dates its allocations (never before the ledger starting date).                                                                      | Rejected if it would strand already-allocated transactions. Allocations follow the new date; history recomputes.                                                                                 |
| Move a fund in the tree  | Changes its parent.                                                                                                                    | Tree balances recompute; moves inside a subtree cancel there. Cycles (a fund under its own descendant) are rejected.                                                                             |
| Delete a fund            | Deletes its allocations. Transactions keep their fund legs, which stop counting (stale references are ignored). Children become roots. | Fund balances and positions recompute from what remains. Consider zeroing the fund with legs first if you want its history to remain meaningful.                                                 |

## Assets, investments, and valuations

| Action                             | Ledger effect                                                                                                                                                       | Derived effect and cautions                                                                                                          |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Create an investment               | Adds a position with an initial quantity.                                                                                                                           | Quantities derive from the initial quantity plus legs.                                                                               |
| Add or update a price              | Records a dated unit-price observation; one per day, the newest same-day price wins.                                                                                | Estimations recompute for the account; history at earlier dates uses earlier observations, unchanged.                                |
| Delete a price                     | Removes the observation.                                                                                                                                            | Estimations fall back to the previous observation, or to absence (never zero).                                                       |
| Delete an investment               | Detaches its references from transactions (legs keep their money amounts, lose their units).                                                                        | Quantities and estimations stop counting it. Recorded buys and sells keep their amounts.                                             |
| Create an asset                    | Adds an object carried at book value.                                                                                                                               | Book value derives from transaction legs referencing it.                                                                             |
| Add a valuation                    | Records a dated whole-asset value.                                                                                                                                  | The estimation and its unrealized difference recompute. Never booked.                                                                |
| Create or edit a depreciation plan | The plan owns a schedule of monthly activities: it creates, updates, or deletes exactly the activities dated today or later; past installments stay frozen history. | Book value, expense flows, and period reports change where installments changed. Editing is the supported way to correct a schedule. |
| Delete a depreciation plan         | Deletes the future activities it still owns; past installments stay, detached from the plan.                                                                        | Consumption already booked remains booked.                                                                                           |
| Delete an asset                    | Detaches its transaction references; deletes its valuations and depreciation plan.                                                                                  | Estimation disappears; book value legs keep their amounts.                                                                           |

## Categories, projects, contacts, counterparties

| Action                                              | Ledger effect                                                                                                                                | Derived effect and cautions                                                                                                          |
| --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Create, rename, or delete a category or subcategory | Changes reporting vocabulary. Deleting sets the label to null on affected activities; deleting a category removes its subcategories with it. | No balance changes. Renames are safe — reports follow the record, not the name.                                                      |
| Create or edit a project                            | Groups activities by scope; its dates describe the project, not the ledger.                                                                  | Activity dates still decide when transactions affect balances.                                                                       |
| Delete a project                                    | Detaches it from activities.                                                                                                                 | Activities keep their financial facts.                                                                                               |
| Create a counterparty                               | Adds the ledger-side identity for shared legs, on an account, optionally with an initial balance and a mapped contact.                       | Shared liability derives from transaction legs naming the counterparty; the stored proportion on account sharing never overrides it. |
| Map a counterparty to a contact                     | Joins the financial identity to a Maille user.                                                                                               | Shared-liability reports can then attribute amounts to that person.                                                                  |
| Delete a counterparty                               | Detaches it from transactions.                                                                                                               | Shared liability for those legs disappears with the reference.                                                                       |
| Add or remove a contact                             | Changes who you are connected to.                                                                                                            | Nothing until sharing or counterparty mappings use the relationship.                                                                 |

## Ledger settings

| Action                   | Effect and cautions                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Change the currency      | Changes the display unit only. No value is converted — change it only when the ledger itself is denominated in the new currency.    |
| Change the starting date | Moves the historical boundary: earlier transactions drop out, later opening balances describe the new boundary. Choose once, early. |
| Change theme             | Stored locally in the browser; no ledger effect.                                                                                    |

## The general rule

Because everything derives, the only irreversible actions are **deletions**
and the only globally impactful settings are the **starting date** and
**opening balances**. For everything else: edit the fact, and every dependent
number follows. If a change feels like it should be impossible, check the
[Ledger concepts](/docs/ledger) section — the refusal is usually protecting an
invariant described there.
