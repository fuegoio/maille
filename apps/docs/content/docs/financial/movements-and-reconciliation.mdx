---
title: Movements & reconciliation
description: Keep bank evidence separate from the ledger, then prove that the two agree.
---

A **movement** is an external statement line: an amount, date, name, and account
reported by a bank or broker. It is evidence that value moved, but it does not
say why. The explanation belongs to one or more activities and their
transactions.

## Why movements are separate

Importing a statement directly as expenses would force a financial
interpretation too early. A card payment might be a purchase, a transfer, a
shared expense, or one part of a larger event. Maille preserves the source line
first and asks you to reconcile it explicitly.

This separation gives you two independently useful records:

- the movement preserves what the institution reported;
- the activity preserves what you concluded happened.

## Reconciliation links

A reconciliation link joins a movement to an activity and records the amount
attributed to that link. The relationship is many-to-many:

- one activity can explain several movements;
- one movement can be split across several activities.

A movement is `completed` when the sum of its linked amounts equals its own
amount exactly. Otherwise it remains `incomplete`.

An activity is complete when the transaction totals for every movement-tracked
account are matched by its linked movement amounts. Accounts with movement
tracking disabled do not require external evidence.

## Example: one payment, two purposes

A `€120` card movement contains groceries for `€80` and household supplies for
`€40`:

1. keep the original movement at `€120`;
2. create two activities with their own expense transactions and categories;
3. link `€80` of the movement to the first activity;
4. link `€40` to the second.

The movement becomes complete at `€120`, while the reporting remains precise.
No statement data is rewritten to fit the categorization.

## Working the queue

Use **Movements → To link** to find imported lines that still need an
explanation. Use **Activities → Needs reconciliation** to find ledger events
whose movement-tracked account totals are not fully backed by statement lines.

<Callout type="info">
  Reconciliation is an equality check, not a confidence score. A record is complete only when the
  linked amounts account for the expected total.
</Callout>

paste-based ingestion.
