Maille is a self-hosted personal finance tool built on a strict double-entry
ledger. Its documentation has two distinct parts: operating the software and
understanding the financial choices it makes.

## What Maille optimizes for

- **Traceability.** A displayed amount should lead back to the transactions that
  produced it.
- **Explicit reconciliation.** A bank movement and its financial explanation
  remain separate until you link them.
- **Purpose without duplication.** Funds describe what money is for without
  pretending that purpose is another bank account.
- **Self-hosted control.** Your ledger lives in your Postgres database. You
  choose the authentication and optional workflow providers connected to it.
